APC Wellness

A hybrid B2B2C/D2C fitness and habit-building platform — sold through employer/health-plan sponsorship as well as direct individual subscriptions. Employers are billed per enrolled/active member; self-pay individuals subscribe directly at plan list price.

Architecture

One shared PostgreSQL warehouse (db_portfolio), company-scoped schemas (*_apc_wellness), dbt Core for transformation, Metabase for dashboards.

  • Finance MRR trend, revenue by segment, revenue by campaign — fully derived, no raw source Built
